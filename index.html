CAREER RESOURCES

Tools for every career journey.

Practical tips whether you're preparing your first application or hiring your next team member.

📄

Build a clear CV or skills profile

Include your work experience, relevant skills, references you have permission to share, and the location where you can work. Even informal work can demonstrate reliability.

Read practical CV tips

Keep a one-page skills summary for casual work. For professional careers, tailor your CV to the role and quantify genuine results. Never invent qualifications.

🤝

Prepare for interviews and job trials

Be ready to explain what you do well, your availability and your expected pay. Ask about duties, hours, safety and when you'll be paid.

Read interview tips

Research the employer when possible. Confirm whether a trial is paid and get the terms in writing. Protect personal documents until a legitimate offer is established.

🛡️

Spot common recruitment scams

Beware of upfront recruitment fees, pressure to send money, fake visa guarantees and requests for passwords or financial credentials.

Read safety checklist

Verify the company through official channels. Independently confirm job addresses. Never send deposits or identification to an unknown recruiter. Report suspicious listings to our team.

🌍

Apply for work across borders

Check visa, work permit, licensing and local employment rules. Availability of a listing does not imply eligibility to work in that country.

Read international job tips

Confirm whether an employer sponsors work authorization, compare local pay periods and living costs, and verify professional licensing requirements.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions & answers.

Is Prowell Jobs only for professional careers?

No. We welcome eligible casual, gig, manual, skilled, domestic, driving and professional vacancies in many countries.

Does Prowell guarantee a job or verify every listing?

No. Vacancies are reviewed before appearing, but a published listing is not a guarantee of legitimacy, employment or payment. Only a specific Verified badge indicates additional employer checks by our team.

Can employers view candidate contact details?

No. Only a limited skills profile is public after consent and approval. Employers may request an introduction through Prowell Jobs; our team checks requests manually.

Should I pay to apply for a job?

Be very cautious. Never send money to an unknown recruiter or pay a fee on the promise of guaranteed employment. Report suspicious requests.

Can I post international vacancies?

Yes, provided the opportunity is lawful and you accurately describe its location, requirements, pay and work authorization needs.

How can I remove my candidate profile?

Use the private withdrawal code provided after submission, or email us requesting removal. Never publish your code.

Privacy Policy

Who we are: Prowell Jobs, prowellofficial@gmail.com. Job posts and application contact details are public after approval. Candidate profiles may display the nickname, skills, country and work preferences provided with consent, but candidate email and phone numbers are not shown publicly. Review names and comments are displayed only after approval.

We also collect job applications (name, contact methods, experience, location and message) privately for administrator review. These are not published or automatically sent to employers. A candidate's contact details may be shared with a verified employer only after further explicit permission. Applicants can withdraw with a private code or request deletion by email. We collect submitted information to operate and moderate listings, review comments, respond to reports and store optional alert-interest signups. We may send transaction-specific email receipts and moderation updates to the private contact address you give us. We do not automatically share applicant data with employers. Saved jobs are stored in your own browser. Alert emails are stored but automated delivery is not yet provided. We do not sell candidate contact details. We share candidate contact information only after manual checks and additional express permission from that candidate. Contact us to request correction, unsubscribe from future alerts, or request deletion. Candidate submitters can use a withdrawal code to delete their profiles.

Data is hosted with our infrastructure provider, potentially outside your country. Retention, applicable privacy rights and international transfer requirements vary by location; further compliance measures may be needed before broader launch.
