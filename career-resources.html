Free career guidance

Every step forward starts with the right information.

From casual shifts to professional careers, use these practical guides to prepare a stronger application, find suitable opportunities, and stay safe.

These guides are general advice, not a guarantee of employment. Always follow the employer's specific instructions and local requirements.

01 · Applications

Build a clear CV or résumé

  1. Start with your name, professional contact details and location; leave out sensitive identification numbers.
  2. Write a short summary of the type of work you can do.
  3. List relevant experience, including informal work, volunteering, projects and apprenticeships when applicable.
  4. Show results where possible, such as customers served, deliveries completed or teams supported.
  5. Include relevant certifications and skills, then proofread and save a PDF copy.
02 · Interviews

Prepare to speak with employers

  1. Read the vacancy again and learn what the employer actually does.
  2. Prepare two or three examples showing reliability, teamwork and problem solving.
  3. Practice explaining your experience clearly and briefly.
  4. Check the interview time, location or video link; plan transport or internet access.
  5. Ask about responsibilities, working hours, pay terms and next steps.
03 · Finding work

Make your job search more effective

  1. Search by location, work type and skills rather than relying only on a job title.
  2. Consider entry-level, part-time, contract and casual opportunities where suitable.
  3. Check the closing date and required qualifications before applying.
  4. Adapt your application to the role; avoid sending unrelated information.
  5. Keep a personal record of applications, dates and employer responses.
04 · Staying safe

Recognize recruitment scams

  • Be cautious when someone guarantees a job in exchange for an upfront fee.
  • Never share passwords, one-time login codes or banking PINs with a recruiter.
  • Verify the employer through independent contact channels when in doubt.
  • Do not send identity documents to unverified contacts or unofficial messaging accounts.
  • Watch for vague roles, pressure to act immediately and requests to move money.
05 · All career paths

Showcase practical and informal experience

Driving, home support, cleaning, construction, hospitality, delivery, retail and other hands-on roles involve valuable skills.

  • Describe tasks you perform consistently and safely.
  • Mention equipment, languages, permits or tools relevant to the role.
  • Include verified references only with the person's permission.
  • Ask employers to confirm duties, compensation and schedules in writing.
06 · Next steps

Follow up professionally

  1. Use the contact method stated in the job listing.
  2. Wait until the employer's stated response period has passed.
  3. Send one short, polite follow-up including the job title and date applied.
  4. Continue applying elsewhere while awaiting a response.
  5. Keep copies of any agreed offer and verify conditions before starting work.
